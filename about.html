About GreenHeart SE

Transforming agricultural waste into climate-positive solutions for a sustainable future

GreenHeart SE is a pioneering agritech and circular economy enterprise based in Ghana. The company transforms agricultural and forestry waste into high-value, climate-resilient products that support sustainable farming, environmental restoration, and carbon removal. By leveraging advanced green technologies, GreenHeart SE contributes to soil regeneration, food security, and climate change mitigation while empowering local communities and farmers.

Our Innovative Products

Biochar Soil Conditioner

Enhances soil fertility, water retention, and crop yield while sequestering carbon.

Biochar-Based Animal Feed

Improves digestion and reduces methane emissions in livestock.

BioCHARGE

Organic pest repellent and plant growth enhancer for sustainable agriculture.

Activated Biochar (Upcoming)

For water filtration, mining, and pharmaceutical applications.

Carbon Credits

Verified carbon removals through biochar production and application.

Our Vision & Mission

Vision

To be Ghana's circular bioeconomy powerhouse by 2035 - regenerating 2 million hectares, capturing 2.5 million tons of CO₂ annually, and transforming 1 million tons of waste into climate-positive products.

Mission

Turning waste into wealth - GreenHeart SE delivers regenerative bio-based solutions that restore land, remove carbon, and empower millions with green livelihoods.

Strategic Goals

Waste-to-Value Transformation

Convert 1 million tons of biomass annually by 2045.

Land Restoration

Restore 2 million hectares using biochar-based soil amendments by 2045.

Carbon Removal

Capture over 2.5 million tons of CO₂-equivalent emissions yearly.

Livelihood Creation

Empower 2 million active users of regenerative solutions.

Market Leadership

Become Ghana's #1 circular bioeconomy enterprise by 2030.

Innovation and R&D

Invest in innovation to enhance product efficiency and impact.

Partnerships

Collaborate with key stakeholders to scale sustainable solutions.

Our Leadership

Caleb Lomo - Founder & Chief Growth Officer

Caleb Lomo is the visionary Founder and Chief Growth Officer (CGO) of GreenHeart SE, a leading enterprise at the forefront of Ghana's regenerative agriculture and climate innovation movement. With over a decade of experience in Project Management, Agribusiness Development, Food Systems Transformation, and Social Entrepreneurship, Caleb brings a unique blend of technical expertise, grassroots insight, and strategic leadership.

A staunch advocate for climate justice, decarbonization, and rural economic transformation, Caleb's mission is to build resilient ecosystems and inclusive green economies across Africa. His work is deeply informed by systems thinking and a strong belief in nature-based solutions.

Under Caleb's leadership, GreenHeart SE has emerged as a catalyst for regenerative impact—converting agricultural and forestry waste into high-value, climate-positive products such as biochar, wood vinegar, and bio-based feed.
